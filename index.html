Jefferson Parish Council Launches Independent Engineering Audit on Smart Water Meter Billing Spikes

Following widespread complaints from residents in Metairie and Kenner regarding sudden quadrupled monthly water charges, the Jefferson Parish Council has commissioned an independent technical audit into newly installed automated meter sensors.

Why It Matters:

New Orleans Formally Awarded Legal Ownership of 50,000-Acre Coastal Wisner Trust Lands

After years of litigation, the civil district court has ruled that the City of New Orleans is the sole rightful owner of the historic Edward Wisner Donation lands, securing tens of millions in annual revenue dedicated to municipal infrastructure.

📌 Click to expand key takeaways directly
Why It Matters:

The Wisner Trust originally encompassed over 50,000 acres of oil-producing and coastal real estate across Lafourche, St. John, and Jefferson parishes. The expiration of the 100-year trust led to a contested dispute over whether revenues belonged to the city or private heirs.

Important Takeaways:
  • The Core Court Ruling: The judge decreed that the donation trust had legally terminated and that all real property and oil royalties permanently vest directly in the City of New Orleans as the sole beneficiary.
  • Financial Impact for Citizens: The city will now directly capture an estimated $8M–$12M annually without third-party management fees. City leadership has pledged these funds directly to drainage pump modernization and neighborhood road repairs.
  • Environmental Significance: The land holdings encompass critical coastal wetlands along the Barataria basin, allowing New Orleans to spearhead direct coastal restoration and hurricane storm surge protection projects.

Major domestic and international car manufacturers are expanding their lineups with Extended-Range EVs—vehicles driven 100% by electric motors but equipped with a compact gasoline generator for total ranges exceeding 700 miles.

📌 Click to expand key takeaways directly
